NOTICES AND INFORMATION

KittenRemoteControl uses .NET 10 runtime libraries supplied by the game and references
StarMap.API 0.4.8 and Harmony supplied by the installed StarMap loader. These assemblies
are not distributed in the mod ZIP.

StarMap: https://github.com/StarMapLoader/StarMap
Harmony: https://github.com/pardeike/Harmony
.NET: https://github.com/dotnet/runtime

Game assemblies (KSA, Brutal and Planet) are proprietary, referenced for compilation
from an existing installation, and are not distributed.

Grapevine and Microsoft.Extensions packages from older versions are no longer used.
Legacy license files are retained for historical reference, not as a dependency list.
